# Wetness

Colonists, their clothes, and the things they leave outside get wet and take time to dry. Wetness is a 0 to 1 value on a colonist (and later on items and ground) that rises in rain, when wading a stream, and when washing, and falls over time, faster near a fire, in sun, or indoors. A wet colonist loses heat faster, so wetness is mostly felt through temperature: wet and cold is miserable and eventually dangerous, wet on a hot day is a relief. Wet clothes stay wet longer than skin. A roof stops rain, which makes shelter matter before walls do. Rain comes from weather.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): "soaked" thought; worse sleep when wet.
- Temperature, [Fire](./fire.md) (wet things don't ignite, fire dries), seasonal climate.
