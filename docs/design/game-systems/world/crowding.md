# Crowding

How many people are around a colonist, against how many they can stand. Outdoors it's the count of colonists within a few metres; indoors it's the room's occupants against its floor area, so six people in a hut is crowded and six in a hall is not. Each colonist has a preferred range set by personality: a loner is strained by company a sociable colonist needs, and too few people around is its own penalty (isolation). Crowding is measured over time, not at an instant, so passing someone in a doorway costs nothing and sharing a small room every night does. It gives the player a reason to build more than one room and to care who bunks with whom.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): crowded and isolated thoughts; raises the weight of social behaviors (snipe, argue) and of wandering off.
- [Rooms](./rooms.md), [Noise](./noise.md), colonist sleep.
