# Crowding

How many people are around a colonist, against how many they can stand. Outdoors it's the count of colonists within a few metres; indoors it's the room's occupants against its floor area, so six people in a hut is crowded and six in a hall is not. Each colonist has a preferred range set by their Social Need ([Attributes](../colonists/attributes.md)): a colonist with low Social Need is strained by company that one with high Social Need requires, and too few people around is its own penalty (isolation). There is no separate tolerance attribute. Crowding is measured over time, not at an instant, so passing someone in a doorway costs nothing and sharing a small room every night does. It gives the player a reason to build more than one room and to care who bunks with whom.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): crowded and isolated thoughts; raises the weight of social behaviors (snipe, argue) and of wandering off.
- [Rooms](./rooms.md), [Noise](./noise.md), colonist sleep.
