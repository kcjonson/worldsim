# Roofs

Every closed room is indoors ([enclosed look](./building-construction.md#enclosed-look)), and roofs refine that with a roof tool, with which the player says which spaces have a roof. A roofed space is indoors. A walled space the player leaves without a roof is open-air: a courtyard, an animal pen, a courtyard in the middle of a building. Roofs can also cover spaces that walls do not enclose. The enclosed look, and later lighting, follow the roof.
