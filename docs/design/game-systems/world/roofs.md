# Roofs

The player says which spaces have a roof, with a roof tool. A roofed space is indoors, and a walled space with no roof is open-air: a courtyard, an animal pen, a courtyard in the middle of a building. Roofs can also cover spaces that walls do not enclose. The [enclosed look](./building-construction.md#enclosed-look), and later lighting, follow which spaces are indoors.
