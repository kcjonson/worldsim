# Noise

Work is loud and people need quiet. A noise is an emitter with a loudness and a radius, either continuous (a crafting station in use, a fire, rain on a roof) or a one-off event (a tree falling, an argument, a fight). What a colonist hears is the loudest sources in range with distance falloff, cut down by each wall between them and the source. Noise wakes sleepers and lowers sleep quality, makes conversation and rest worse, and carries news: a colonist who hears a fight knows there was one without seeing it. Colonists differ in how much noise bothers them, through traits (a light sleeper wakes at less, a heavy sleeper sleeps through more); there is no tolerance attribute. This pushes the player toward layout decisions, bedrooms away from the workshop.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): sleep interruptions, "couldn't hear myself think"; arguments and fights reach witnesses by sound.
- Colonist sleep, [Crowding](./crowding.md).
