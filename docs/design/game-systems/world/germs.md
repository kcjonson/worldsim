# Germs (hygiene and contamination)

The Hygiene need says how dirty a colonist feels; germs say what that dirt does. Contamination is a value on entities: hands, food, water, surfaces, beds, and bio piles. It starts at sources (waste, rot, dirty water, wounds), transfers on contact (an unwashed colonist handling food, a soiled bed), and decays or is removed by washing, cooking, and cleaning. Eating, drinking, or sleeping on something contaminated carries a chance of sickness scaled by the amount. Colonists can't see germs, only their signs (smell, visible filth), so sanitation is something the colony learns the hard way and then builds for: latrines away from water, a wash spot by the kitchen.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): "dirty hands in the food" and "soiled a bed" are contamination events; sickness is a mood penalty.
- [Smell](./smell.md), cooking and water, bio locations.
