# Fire

Things burn. A fire is a lit entity (a campfire, a torch, a burning wall or tree) that gives off light and heat, consumes fuel, and can ignite flammable things close to it. Every asset and structure material carries a flammability value; ignition is a chance per hour that rises with heat and proximity and falls with wetness, so rain and wet ground suppress it. Spread runs entity to entity by distance, never across a grid. Colonists see fire, react to it (flee, fetch water, or stand and watch, by personality), and are hurt by standing in it. Burned things lose integrity and end as ash or charred remains.

## Consumers

- [Mood](../colonists/mood.md): arson needs a lit fire in reach; a campfire is a comfort source.
- Lighting (light emitters), [Wetness](./wetness.md), [Smell](./smell.md) (smoke).
